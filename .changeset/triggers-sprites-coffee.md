Triggers know the newer things: events `throw`, `explode`, `breed`, `wreck`, `summon` and `touch`, conditions `ammo`, `team` and `riding`, actions `callTeam` and `hazard` (a trap). Objects (items with `pickup: none`) run their `do` when touched (`every`) and take sprites; hideouts take a `sprite`. The editor's flow view knows all of it, shows cards for hideouts, and has a `supportUrl` option for a "Buy me a coffee" link (also `supportUrl` in the Payload plugin). READMEs updated.
