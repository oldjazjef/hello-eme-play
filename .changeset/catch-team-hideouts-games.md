Catching and a team: throwables with `catch` (balls), `catchRate` on entities, a team that fights for the figure (`keys.party`, actions `recruit` and `healParty`, `evolve.kills`), `hideouts` and `hideIn` (monsters hide until the figure walks through), trigger events `catch`, `escape`, `encounter`, `evolve` and `faint`, sprites with `rotate: false`. Ten complete example games (`game-*` presets) with pictures, among them the Pokémon-style "Ememons".
