Key triggers: a trigger can wait for a key (`"on": "key"`, `"keys": ["KeyB"]`), so people bind a key to any action: press B to spawn a blob, V to switch the view, H to heal. `configProblems` flags a key trigger without keys and the reserved Esc; the editor shows the keys in the trigger's sentence.
