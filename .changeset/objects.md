Items can be objects with properties: `pickup: none` (stays), `solid` (blocks figures, monsters and vehicles), `hides` (covers what stands in it), `damage` and `damageWho` (hurts or, negative, heals by touching), new built-in looks `wall`, `spikes`, `bush`, `puddle`, and `itemSpawns[].initial` to scatter them over the page. All example games use objects.
